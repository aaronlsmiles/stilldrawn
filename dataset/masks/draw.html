How to draw

Click to place points; click the first point, double-click, or press Enter or C to close the outline. Or press and drag to lasso a shape in one stroke. Esc drops an unfinished outline. A row can hold several shapes. Every change saves itself; an outline still open when you change rows is closed if it has 3 points, dropped if not.

Keys: Z undo; N nothing may move (asks first if the row has shapes); X clear the row (asks first); H hide or show masks; ← or , previous row; → or . next row; U next row still to do. Keys do not repeat when held, except the arrows. Outlines with no area (all points in a line) are refused.
